Statement Cue Analyzer

陳述線索分析器 · research & education prototype

Behavioral cues only — NOT a lie detector. Stress ≠ lying. Fatigue, lighting, eyewear and camera angle all change these numbers.

Not for hiring, legal review, or accusations against a specific person.

僅輸出可觀測線索，無法判斷是否說謊。禁止用於面試、法律審查、針對特定人物的指控。

Video is processed in your browser and never uploaded. 影片只在你的裝置上處理，不會上傳。

[Compliance] Behavioral cue only, NOT lie detection. Stress ≠ Lying. Low face coverage means the numbers are unreliable.

Method: MediaPipe Face Landmarker, eye aspect ratio (EAR) sampled at 30 fps. The threshold adapts to each video: 75% of the open-eye baseline (80th-percentile EAR). A blink is a closure of 2 samples to 0.5 s; longer closures (looking down, squinting) are reported separately. The detection model is downloaded once from Google's CDN; your video stays on your device.
